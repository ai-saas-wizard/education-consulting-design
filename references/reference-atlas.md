## Aligned — editorial coaching

[Contact sheet](screenshots/aligned-contact-sheet.jpg). Overall: ivory and warm gray surfaces, dark gray type, pill actions, small tracked eyebrows, large lightweight sans headings with selective heavier words. Founder and client photography anchors the page; generous pauses separate narrative sections. Header stays visible during scrolling. Adapt the human credibility and rhythm, while keeping the main offer/action explicit.

## Digital Launchpad — product-led academy

[Contact sheet](screenshots/launchpad-contact-sheet.jpg). Overall: near-black background, orange/red emphasis and ambient glows, condensed uppercase headings, rounded dark panels, product UI and instructor imagery. The recurring product view makes an intangible membership visible. Prefer bounded curriculum detail over excessive scrolling.

| Section | Saved frames | Observed composition and reusable lesson |

## Metabolic Makeover — additions to the design vocabulary

[Hero](screenshots/mma-hero.png). Observed layout: light navigation, charcoal split hero, large photographic half, white heading and cool muted action. The page proceeds through a video-led method, a numbered work plan with stated engagement duration, delivery/support inclusions, fit criteria, and application. This is a useful consulting pattern: tell visitors how the engagement works and what they must commit before asking for a call. Prefer shorter left-aligned copy over the dense justified hero paragraph. Medical mechanisms and guaranteed outcomes are publisher claims, not reusable facts. Team and testimonial pages provide deeper evidence through navigation. [Source](https://www.metabolicmakeoveracademy.org/)

## IELTS Advantage — additions to the design vocabulary

[Hero](screenshots/ielts-hero.png). White header, navy hero, orange action, centered promise and compact credibility strip. The homepage sequences student difficulty, course inclusions, a feedback process, video proof, guarantees, founder/team credibility, then lower-commitment diagnostic and free-learning paths. The transferable idea is readiness-based routing with a single dominant course action. The method explains the service behind the content. Keep guarantee summaries aligned with actual terms; a homepage slogan is not enough. The linked course page has a separate long-form sales journey. [Source](https://www.ieltsadvantage.com/)
