# Conversion principles adapted from the supplied guide

The original guide is retained unchanged through Part VIII and its final principles; a clearly separated Part IX adds this project's findings. It is research, not audited conversion-rate evidence. Preserve the useful logic without treating every operator's claims or funnel tactics as proven.

## Design decisions

1. Match the visitor's identity and stage early. A broad education hub may route multiple stages; an application page may have one narrow audience.
2. Explain the mechanism through deliverables, sample work, a lesson preview, or a short process. A named method without component logic adds little.
3. Put relevant proof beside the claim. Prefer baseline, intervention, timeframe, and outcome over unsupported totals. If proof is unavailable, design a real demonstration slot instead of fabricated testimonials.
4. Reduce next-step ambiguity: what the action does, what information it requests, whether it costs money, and what follows it.
5. One visually dominant action per screen does not mean one link on the entire site. Resources, policies, and a quieter diagnostic path can serve different readiness levels.
6. Cold visitors often need explanation before qualification; warm visitors may accept a faster path. Do not infer traffic warmth solely from industry.
7. Video should enhance comprehension. Provide a descriptive poster, visible controls, and equivalent core information in text. Do not make enrollment depend on watching a long video.
8. Show actual delivery: curriculum, feedback cadence, coaching access, tools, expected effort, and duration. Separate immediate access from time-gated bonuses.
9. Qualification should describe fit respectfully. Collect only fields that support the next step. Explain a lengthy application before the click.
10. State known billing and risk terms close to the decision. Do not invent discounts, countdowns, scarcity, guarantees, accreditation, or medical/income outcomes.
11. Judge proposed experiments on qualified applications, booked/showed calls, enrollment, or activation as appropriate. Colors alone do not establish conversion performance.

## Adaptable section sequences

**Consulting/coaching:** concise hero → matched proof → situation/constraint → method → deliverables → relevant case → people → engagement process → fit → investment/terms → FAQ → application action.

**Course/membership:** outcome + product preview → audience fit → curriculum/sample → learning method/support → instructor credibility → proof → inclusions and access schedule → pricing/terms → FAQ → enrollment.

**Education hub:** clear educational promise → resource routing → expert method → course value → learner proof → teacher credibility → primary course action → quieter diagnostic/free-learning path.

Omit, merge, or reorder blocks based on the actual decision. A small offer should not inherit a 20-section funnel just because a reference has one.
